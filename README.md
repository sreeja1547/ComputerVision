# ProjectPulse AI — Industrial Safety Intelligence System### AI-Powered PPE Detection, Workplace Hazard Monitoring, and Safety Risk AssessmentProjectPulse AI is an AI-powered industrial safety monitoring system designed to help identify potential workplace hazards in construction and industrial environments. It combines computer vision, object detection, configurable safety rules, risk assessment, and incident monitoring in a unified application.The system is designed to analyse images and video footage, identify supported objects and personal protective equipment (PPE), assess configured safety conditions, and present findings through a dashboard.> **Project status:** Prototype / development project. OverviewIndustrial workplaces contain several potential hazards, including missing personal protective equipment, unsafe movement through restricted areas, and interactions between workers and machinery.Traditional safety inspections often depend on manual observation. Continuous monitoring can be difficult when multiple workers, machines, and work zones must be inspected simultaneously.ProjectPulse AI explores how computer vision and configurable safety rules can support workplace inspections.The application aims to:1. Analyse images and video footage using an object-detection model.2. Identify people and supported PPE classes.3. Evaluate detections against configured safety rules.4. Calculate a rule-based safety risk score.5. Record potential incidents for review.6. Present detection and risk information through a dashboard.7. Support further integration with camera streams and industrial monitoring systems where configured and tested.The system is intended to **assist safety personnel, not replace qualified human inspections or established workplace safety procedures**.---## ?? Problem StatementManual industrial safety inspections can be time-consuming and may not provide continuous visibility across a large worksite.Potential safety issues can go unnoticed when inspections are infrequent or when monitoring many workers simultaneously.An AI-assisted monitoring system can help prioritise inspection activities by identifying possible hazards in images and video footage.The challenge is to build a system that combines object detection, configurable safety rules, risk assessment, and incident management while minimising false alarms and missed hazards.---## ?? Objectives- Develop an AI-assisted industrial safety monitoring application.- Use YOLO-based computer vision for supported object-detection tasks.- Identify PPE-related conditions using model predictions and configured rules.- Evaluate potential hazards using a configurable risk engine.- Support the identification and review of safety incidents.- Present safety information through a dashboard.- Improve traceability through incident records.- Provide a foundation for future real-time monitoring and predictive safety analytics.---## ?
